Why this points to stock, not ads
Our campaigns target buyers across all of India in the same way. If the ads had stopped working, every region would have dropped by a similar amount. Instead, the drop is about 2–3 times bigger exactly where buyers lost their nearby stock. Kerala, the only region that gained nearby stock, fell only 18%. Ads bring the buyer to the product page; when delivery is slow because stock is far away, fewer of them buy.

How to read this
In Mar–Jul stock roughly followed orders: the big regions (Maharashtra, Hyderabad, Bengaluru) each had a fair share of stock nearby. In October the picture flipped: Tamil Nadu holds 63% of the stock for 10% of orders, while Maharashtra, Hyderabad and Bengaluru together hold 20% of the stock for 56% of orders. North and East India have never had stock nearby.
